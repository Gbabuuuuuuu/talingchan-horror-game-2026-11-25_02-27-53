PROTOTYPE DESIGN RECORD - 25 NOVEMBER 2026
All decisions below apply to gameplay experiments. They are not final production design.

LATEST USER REQUIREMENT
Every design-facing system must expose its tunable data in Unity Inspector.
Use serialized components / configuration assets, organized headings, useful labels,
tooltips, validation, and visible debug state. Do not hard-code balance, quest content,
item definitions, recipes, save points, audio references, or event timing.

AGREED FOR THE PROTOTYPE
- Single-player PC, keyboard and mouse, first-person village horror.
- Explore every house freely. Some houses have items, some have none.
- Gather items and craft. No level / hunger / health-meter system requested.
- Limited inventory: 10 slots; item stacking is allowed.
- Maximum quantity per stack is NOT specified yet; make it configurable per item.
- Quest wheel / inventory blocks player movement; ghosts and the world continue.
- Complete every story quest before the final-boss reveal.
- Quest tracker at screen edge, combined with narrative / environmental clues.
- Missing a clue leaves a quest incomplete; revisiting and NPC hints can recover it.
- Items do not respawn in the same run. New-run item placements may be randomized.
- Ordinary ghost appearances are primarily atmospheric and non-damaging.
- Stabber NPC: candidate trigger is 120 seconds of AFK or entry into forbidden zones.
- Exclude reading, dialogue, inventory, and cutscenes from the AFK timer.
- Death returns the player to the latest checkpoint.
- Checkpoint location is deferred; it may be a house or another location.
- Flashlight remains important. Phone gameplay is deferred.
- Crafting / charm details and final boss story remain undecided.
- Visual art and cutscene production are deferred. Temporary audio may be sourced.

INSPECTOR CONFIGURATION REQUIRED
- Inventory capacity, item stack limits, item categories, drop/use permissions.
- Item definitions, spawn points / pools, required quest items, new-run seed options.
- Quest descriptions, objectives, dependencies, completion events and rewards.
- Crafting stations, ingredient lists, recipe outputs, blessing interactions.
- AFK duration, excluded player states, forbidden areas, pursuit / detection settings.
- Death / respawn settings, checkpoint transforms, saved state categories.
- Interaction distances, input bindings where applicable, movement tuning.
- Ghost spawn points, triggers, delays, durations, event frequency and enable switches.
- Audio clips, volume, pitch, distance / falloff, loop and transition settings.
- UI text, layout tuning, temporary-content switches, test / reset actions.
