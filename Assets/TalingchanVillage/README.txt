BAN RIM KHLONG - playable village prototype
Scene: Scenes/BanRimKhlong.unity
10 furnished enterable homes, grocery, market, community hall, road network, drainage, power poles and wires, gardens, water tank, pond and fields.
Temple compound is OUTSIDE the village to the north; follow the road through the tree-lined approach. Includes an enterable ordination hall, Buddha altar, chedi and bell pavilion.
WASD move, mouse look, Shift run, Space jump, F draw/stow flashlight, G flashlight power, R respawn, Esc cursor.
T incoming call, E answer, Q decline/end. Automatic demo calls are disabled in this exploration scene.
Models and PBR sources: see Sources/CREDITS.txt. Thai font: Sources/Fonts/OFL.txt.
Lighting uses Forward+ and a runtime shadow budget. Vegetation includes lower-detail distance models.
This is a realistic textured prototype, not a survey or reconstruction of a real Thai village or temple.
